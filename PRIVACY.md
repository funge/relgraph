# privacy

updated: 2026-11-11

relgraph is developed by John Funge. no account or sign-in is needed to use the
app.

## on your device

relgraph calculates graphs on your device. saved graphs, preferences and recovery
drafts are stored locally. recovery drafts include your last submitted graph and
unfinished formula and view edits.

local diagnostic output can include formulas, view bounds and rendering results.
it is not a developer-operated analytics service. check any logs before choosing
to share them for support.

## sharing and device services

copying uses the system clipboard. importing, exporting and sharing use the
system's file and sharing interfaces. saving an image can use your photo library.
you choose what to share and where to send it; the selected service's privacy
policy applies.

device backups, clipboard synchronization and system diagnostics are managed by
your device and platform settings. deleting a graph or app data does not delete
copies you have exported, shared or backed up.

## support and links

support is provided through [GitHub issues](https://github.com/funge/relgraph/issues).
information you submit there, including your GitHub identity and issue content,
is visible to others. provide only what is needed to explain the problem; do not
post private graph content, credentials or other sensitive information.

support discussions help investigate and resolve reported problems. GitHub hosts
and retains issue content under its
[privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
you can edit your own posts and request removal of content through GitHub's
support tools. removing a post may not remove copies held by other people.

opening an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy policy.

## questions

for general questions about this policy, open a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/funge/relgraph/issues). this is a public channel,
not private correspondence. do not include sensitive details.
